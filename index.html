How LaTeX to Word works

  1. 1 Paste ChatGPT or LaTeX content
  2. 2 Preview updates automatically
  3. 3 Download .docx and open in Microsoft Word

Supports \[...\], \(...\), $$...$$, and $...$ math. HTML and TSV tables paste as Markdown pipes. Draft autosaves locally. Shortcuts: Ctrl+Enter refresh, Ctrl+Shift+D download .docx, Ctrl+Shift+H download HTML, Ctrl+Shift+T download Word with plain-text math.

About this LaTeX to Word converter

LaTeX to Word (LaTeX2Word) turns ChatGPT output, Markdown, and LaTeX into a Microsoft Word .docx with editable native equations — not screenshots. Ideal for students, researchers, and anyone who needs LaTeX math in Word without manual retyping. Your document is processed on your device. Conversion is not uploaded.

This is enabled only when the convert has a warning or error. The report includes your pasted text and the equations that failed.
