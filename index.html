About this LaTeX to Word converter

        

          LaTeX to Word (LaTeX2Word) turns pasted LaTeX, ChatGPT output, and Markdown into a
          Microsoft Word .docx with editable native equations, not
          screenshots. Paste any LaTeX content, or extract plain text from an image in your browser. PDFs are
          not yet supported. Your content stays on your device; OCR runs locally. Nothing from the editor is
          uploaded unless you click Send error report.
        

        

          
          

            
            

              You can send a report even when Warnings and Failed are 0. Sending includes the pasted
              document, any failed equations, and your note. Nothing is uploaded until you click Send.
