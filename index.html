LaTeX to Word

Convert ChatGPT, Markdown, and LaTeX into a Word document with editable equations.

Your document is processed locally in your browser.

How it works

  1. Paste ChatGPT or LaTeX content
  2. Click Convert
  3. Download .docx and open in Microsoft Word

Paste ChatGPT math, Markdown, or LaTeX. Copy-button Markdown from ChatGPT usually uses \[...\] and \(...\). Dollar signs, $$, and conservative [...] display math are also supported. Tables copied as HTML or tab-separated text are converted to Markdown pipes so preview and Word keep a real grid.
